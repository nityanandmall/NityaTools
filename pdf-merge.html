Merge PDF Online

Select two or more PDF files and combine them into one PDF in the order selected.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Select at least two PDF files.
  2. Choose them in the order you want them combined.
  3. Press Merge PDFs, then download the new file.

Browser-based PDF merging

The selected PDFs are processed by the PDF library running in your browser rather than by NityaTools application server code. The required PDF library itself is loaded from a third-party CDN, so an internet connection is required to load it.

Limitations

Password-protected, encrypted or damaged PDFs may not be supported. For important documents, check the downloaded PDF before deleting or replacing your originals.
