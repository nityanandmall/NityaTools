🏷️ Discount Calculator

How to calculate a discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100. Subtract that saving from the original price to get the final sale price.

Discount amount = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100.
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount.

Example

A 20% discount on a price of 100 saves 20, so the final price is 80. The calculator works with any currency because it calculates numeric values rather than applying a currency-specific rule.
