🔋 Capacitor Energy Calculator

Energy = ½ × C × V²

Capacitor energy formula

The energy stored in an ideal capacitor is calculated with E = ½ × C × V², where E is energy in joules, C is capacitance in farads and V is voltage in volts.

Microfarads to farads

This calculator accepts capacitance in microfarads (µF). It divides the entered value by 1,000,000 to convert microfarads to farads before applying the energy formula.

Example

A 1000 µF capacitor charged to 12 V stores approximately 0.072 joules in the ideal calculation. Real components have tolerances, voltage limits, losses and safety considerations, so use component datasheets for practical circuit design.
