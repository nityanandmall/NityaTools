NITYA TOOLS

About NityaTools

NityaTools is a growing online toolbox designed to make everyday calculations and digital tasks simple, fast and accessible. The website brings together calculators and utilities for electronics, electrical work, solar and energy, finance, students, developers, unit conversion, images, PDFs and everyday use.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a useful global tools platform that can serve people from different countries and, over time, support tools and information relevant to their local needs and languages.

How the Tools Work

Many NityaTools utilities run directly in the user's browser. Calculators provide estimates based on the information entered by the user. Some tools may rely on third-party browser libraries or services where required.

Accuracy and Responsibility

We work to make our tools useful and understandable, but results should be treated as informational estimates. For financial, electrical, engineering, legal, medical, tax or other important decisions, users should verify results with official information or a qualified professional.

Growing Platform

NityaTools is actively being developed. New tools, country-specific utilities and improvements may be added over time.
