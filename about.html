About NityaTools

NityaTools is a growing online toolbox designed to make everyday calculations and digital tasks simple, fast and accessible. It brings together calculators and utilities for electronics, electrical work, solar and energy, finance, students, developers, unit conversion, images, PDFs and everyday use.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a useful global tools platform that can serve people from different countries and, over time, support tools and information relevant to local needs and languages.

How the Tools Work

Many NityaTools utilities run directly in the user's browser. Calculators provide results based on the values and assumptions shown on each tool page. Some features use third-party browser libraries or services when required; relevant tool pages may provide additional details.

Accuracy and Responsibility

We work to make tools useful and understandable, but results should be treated as informational estimates unless a tool clearly states otherwise. For financial, electrical, engineering, legal, medical, tax or other important decisions, verify the result with official information or an appropriately qualified professional.

Privacy-conscious Utilities

Where practical, file and text utilities are designed to process data in the browser. Some tools require external libraries or services, so users should review the relevant tool description and our Privacy Policy.

Growing Platform

NityaTools is actively being developed. New tools, country-specific utilities and usability improvements may be added over time. Browse Categories or view All Tools.
