Split / Extract PDF Pages

Enter pages such as 1,3,5-8 to create a new PDF containing only those pages.

How PDF page extraction works

Choose a PDF and enter individual page numbers or ranges separated by commas. Duplicate page selections are removed and page numbers outside the document are ignored.

Processing is performed in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library loaded from jsDelivr. Encrypted, password-protected, damaged or unusually complex PDFs may fail to process.
