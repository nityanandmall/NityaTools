🎂 Age Calculator

Calculate your age from a date of birth to today or another selected date.

Select your date of birth.

How the age calculator works

Enter a date of birth and choose the date on which you want to calculate the age. The calculator compares the calendar dates and displays completed years, remaining months and days. It also shows the total number of days between the two selected dates.

Age in years, months and days

Calendar age is not calculated by simply dividing the number of days by 365 because months have different lengths and leap years contain an extra day. This tool uses calendar year, month and day values for the detailed age result.

Useful for forms and eligibility checks

You can select a past or future cutoff date to estimate age on a specific date, which can be useful when checking age-related requirements. Always verify official eligibility rules separately because organizations may apply their own cutoff-date or rounding rules.
