How to calculate an average

The arithmetic mean is found by adding all values and dividing the sum by the number of values.

Average = Sum of values ÷ Number of values.

Example

For 10, 20, 30 and 40, the sum is 100 and the count is 4. The average is therefore 100 ÷ 4 = 25.

What this tool shows

Along with the average, the calculator displays the total sum and count so you can quickly verify the calculation. It accepts positive numbers, negative numbers and decimals.
