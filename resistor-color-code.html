How a 4-band resistor code works

The first two bands provide the significant digits, the third band is the multiplier, and the fourth band gives tolerance. For example, Brown–Black–Red–Gold represents 10 × 10² = 1,000 Ω or 1 kΩ with ±5% tolerance.

This calculator covers the displayed standard 4-band options. Always verify component markings and required power rating before using a resistor in a practical circuit.
