Ohm's Law formulas

Voltage: V = I × R
Current: I = V ÷ R
Resistance: R = V ÷ I
Power: P = V × I

How to use this calculator

Enter exactly two known electrical values. The missing value is calculated using Ohm's Law, and power is then calculated from voltage and current.

Example

If a circuit has 12 V across a 6 Ω resistor, the current is 2 A and the electrical power is 24 W.

Real circuits can include reactive components, changing loads and temperature effects, so use appropriate engineering methods for designs where safety or compliance matters.
