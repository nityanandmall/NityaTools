📅 Date Difference Calculator

Select two dates.

How this date difference is calculated

The calculator finds the absolute difference between the two selected calendar dates and shows the result in total days, weeks and approximate years.

The years value uses an average year length of 365.2425 days, so it is an approximation rather than a calendar age calculation.

When to use it

Use this tool for project durations, deadlines, travel intervals or general date comparisons. For exact age in calendar years, months and days, use the Age Calculator instead.
