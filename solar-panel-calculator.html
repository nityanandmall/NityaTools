How solar panel sizing works

A basic solar sizing estimate starts with the amount of energy you use each day. That energy requirement is divided by the average daily peak sun hours and adjusted for expected system losses.

Formula: Required solar watts ≈ Daily energy use ÷ (Peak sun hours × System efficiency).

What are peak sun hours?

Peak sun hours are not the same as total daylight hours. They represent the equivalent number of hours per day when solar irradiance averages about 1,000 W/m². Local weather, season, tilt, shading and location can all affect production.

Why real systems may need extra capacity

Actual solar design should allow for inverter losses, temperature, dust, cable losses, battery charging losses, panel aging and future load growth. Use this result as an initial planning estimate.
