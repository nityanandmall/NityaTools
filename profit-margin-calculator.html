Profit, margin and markup

Profit = Selling Price − Cost. Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of selling price: Profit ÷ Selling Price × 100. Markup measures profit relative to cost: Profit ÷ Cost × 100.

Example

If an item costs 80 and sells for 100, profit is 20, profit margin is 20%, and markup is 25%. The same formulas work with any currency when both values use the same currency.
