🔌 Energy Cost Calculator

How energy use is calculated

Energy (kWh) = Power (W) ÷ 1000 × Hours per day × Days. Estimated energy cost is the calculated kWh multiplied by the cost per kWh you enter.

Example

A 1,000 W appliance used for 5 hours per day for 30 days consumes an estimated 150 kWh. At a rate of 0.15 per kWh, the energy-charge estimate is 22.50 in the same currency as your rate.

Actual bills can differ because of tariffs, slabs, taxes, fixed charges, power variation, standby use and appliance cycling.
