Solar battery sizing formula

The tool multiplies daily energy by autonomy days, then adjusts for usable depth of discharge and system efficiency. Nominal storage = Load energy ÷ (DoD × Efficiency). Battery Ah is nominal watt-hours divided by bank voltage.

This is a storage estimate rather than a complete solar-system design. Battery chemistry, temperature, ageing, discharge rate, inverter losses, BMS limits and manufacturer recommendations should be considered before selecting a real battery bank.
