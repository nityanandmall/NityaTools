Count words and characters instantly

Type or paste text into the box and the counters update automatically. Words are estimated by separating non-empty text at whitespace, while the character count includes spaces and punctuation.

Reading time estimate

The reading-time value uses an approximate rate of 200 words per minute and rounds up to a whole minute. Actual reading speed varies by reader and content complexity.

Common uses

A word counter can help check essays, articles, social posts, descriptions and other text against length requirements. For strict submission rules, verify how the destination defines words and characters.
