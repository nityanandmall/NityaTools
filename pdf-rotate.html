Rotate PDF

Rotate all pages clockwise and download a new PDF.

Rotate every PDF page

The selected rotation is added to each page's existing rotation, modulo 360 degrees. The tool creates a new PDF without changing your original file.

Processing happens in your browser with pdf-lib loaded from jsDelivr. This version rotates all pages together rather than allowing different angles for individual pages. Encrypted or damaged PDFs may not process.
