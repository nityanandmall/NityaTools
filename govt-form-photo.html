📸 Govt Form Photo Resizer


Always check the exact photo requirements of the form before uploading your result.

Resize and center-crop in your browser

The selected image is processed locally with the browser canvas. The tool scales the image to cover the selected dimensions, center-crops the excess area, fills the canvas background white and exports a JPG at approximately 90% quality.

The listed dimensions are convenient presets, not a guarantee that they match a particular government recruitment, examination, passport or application portal. Requirements for dimensions, face position, background, DPI and file size vary, so verify the official instructions for the specific form.
