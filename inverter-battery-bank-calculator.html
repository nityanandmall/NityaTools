How battery-bank sizing works

The load energy is adjusted for inverter efficiency and usable depth of discharge. The tool converts required nominal energy to bank Ah, calculates how many batteries are needed in series for system voltage, then rounds parallel strings upward to meet the Ah target.

Battery chemistry, ageing, temperature, discharge rate, surge loads, cable losses and manufacturer limits can change real backup performance. Use compatible batteries and follow manufacturer guidance for series/parallel configurations.
