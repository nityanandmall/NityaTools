🎓 Marks Percentage Calculator

How to calculate marks percentage

Divide the marks obtained by the maximum or total marks and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100.

Example

If a student scores 425 marks out of 500, the percentage is (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%.

Percentage is not always the same as a grade

Schools, boards, universities and examinations can use different grading, rounding and conversion rules. This tool calculates the mathematical percentage only; use the relevant institution's official rules when a grade, CGPA or eligibility result is required.
