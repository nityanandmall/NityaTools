Supported unit categories

This converter supports length, weight, area, volume, speed, pressure, energy, digital data and temperature conversions.

How unit conversion works

Most categories use a common base unit. The entered value is converted to the base unit and then converted to the destination unit. Temperature uses dedicated Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin formulas because temperature scales include offsets as well as ratios.

Examples

You can convert kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, liters to US gallons, meters per second to kilometers per hour, Celsius to Fahrenheit, kilowatt-hours to joules and gigabytes to megabytes.
