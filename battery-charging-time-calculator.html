🔋 Battery Charging Time Calculator

Estimate charging time using capacity, charger current and efficiency.

Enter values and calculate.

Charging time estimate

The tool uses Time ≈ Battery Capacity ÷ (Charger Current × Efficiency), with efficiency entered as a decimal percentage.

Example

A 150 Ah battery with a 15 A charger at 85% assumed efficiency gives an idealized estimate of about 11.76 hours.

Real charging is often non-linear. Battery chemistry, state of charge, temperature, charger stages, current tapering and battery-management limits can change the actual time. Follow battery and charger manufacturer specifications.
