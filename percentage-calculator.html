How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply it by the number. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

Formula: Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number.

What percent is one number of another?

Divide the first number by the second number and multiply by 100. For example, 50 is 25% of 200.

Formula: Percentage = (First Number ÷ Second Number) × 100.

Percentage increase or decrease

Percentage change compares the difference between a new value and an old value with the absolute old value. A positive result indicates an increase and a negative result indicates a decrease.

Formula: Percentage Change = ((New − Old) ÷ |Old|) × 100.
