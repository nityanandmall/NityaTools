🍽️ Tip Calculator

Works with any currency. Enter the bill, tip percentage and number of people.

How to calculate a tip

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage divided by 100. Add the tip to the bill to get the final total.

Formula: Tip = Bill × Tip % ÷ 100. Total = Bill + Tip.

Split the bill per person

When more than one person is paying, the calculator divides the final total by the number of people to estimate an equal share per person.

Works with any currency

The calculator does not convert currencies. It simply applies the percentage to the amount you enter, so you can use dollars, euros, rupees, dirhams or any other currency consistently.
