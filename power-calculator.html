⚡ Electrical Power Calculator

Power formula

For DC circuits and simple resistive loads, electrical power is P = V × I, where P is watts, V is volts and I is amperes. Divide watts by 1,000 to convert to kilowatts.

AC load note

Real AC power can also depend on power factor and phase configuration. This simple calculator does not apply power factor or three-phase formulas, so use the appropriate electrical calculation for non-resistive or three-phase loads.
