DC voltage drop formula

This calculator uses a two-wire loop: R = 2 × Length × Resistivity ÷ Area, then Voltage Drop = Current × Resistance. It uses approximately 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m for copper and 0.0282 for aluminium.

Actual conductor resistance changes with temperature and installation conditions. Cable selection must also satisfy ampacity, insulation, protection and applicable electrical-code requirements.
