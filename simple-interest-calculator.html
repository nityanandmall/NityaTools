💵 Simple Interest Calculator

Simple interest formula

Interest = Principal × Annual Rate × Time ÷ 100. The total amount is the original principal plus the calculated interest.

Example

For a principal of 10,000 at 5% per year for 2 years, simple interest is 1,000 and the total amount is 11,000.

This calculator assumes the same simple annual rate for the full period and does not compound interest or include fees, taxes or lender-specific rules.
