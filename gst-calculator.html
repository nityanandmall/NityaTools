🇮🇳 GST Calculator India

Quickly add GST to an exclusive price or remove GST from an inclusive price.

How this GST calculator works

For an amount before tax, select “Add GST.” The calculator multiplies the base amount by the selected GST percentage and adds the tax to show the total. To find the base value from a GST-inclusive amount, select “Remove GST.”

GST calculation formulas

Add GST: GST = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100. Total = Base Amount + GST.
Remove GST: Base Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100).

Important note

The available rate buttons are provided for quick calculation. The GST rate and tax treatment applicable to a particular supply can depend on current law, classification, place of supply and other rules. Verify important tax calculations using applicable official information or a qualified tax professional.
