How to calculate battery backup time

This calculator first estimates usable battery energy from voltage and amp-hour capacity, then adjusts it for efficiency and the percentage of battery capacity you plan to use.

Formula: Backup hours ≈ (Voltage × Ah × Efficiency × Usable Capacity) ÷ Load watts.

Example

A 12 V, 150 Ah battery stores about 1,800 Wh before losses. With 85% efficiency and 80% usable capacity, a 600 W load gives an estimated backup of about 2.04 hours.

Why actual backup may differ

Real runtime can change because of battery age and chemistry, temperature, inverter losses, discharge rate, wiring and changing appliance loads. Use the result as a planning estimate rather than a guaranteed runtime.
