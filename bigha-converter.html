Bigha is not a uniform national unit and can vary by district and local custom. Always verify the local land-record standard before legal or property use.

Convert Bigha to other land units

Select a regional preset and enter the number of Bigha. The tool first converts the selected Bigha value to square feet, then derives square meters and acres.

Why Bigha values vary

Unlike square meters or acres, Bigha does not have one uniform value throughout India. Even within a state, local usage can differ. The presets on this page are approximate convenience values and should not be treated as an official land-measurement standard.

For land records and transactions

For registration, sale, mutation, revenue records, surveying or legal documents, use the unit conversion and measurement standard accepted by the relevant local authority or qualified survey professional.
