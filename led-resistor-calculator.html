💡 LED Resistor Calculator

Estimate a series resistor for a single LED.

LED series resistor formula

R = (Vs − Vf) ÷ I, where current I is expressed in amperes. Resistor dissipation is estimated as P = (Vs − Vf) × I.

Example

With a 5 V supply, 2 V LED forward voltage and 20 mA current, the calculated resistance is about 150 Ω and resistor dissipation is about 0.06 W.

The tool rounds its suggested resistor upward in 10 Ω steps; that is only a simple approximation, not a full preferred-value-series selector. LED forward voltage and safe current depend on the actual LED, so check its datasheet and choose a resistor with adequate power rating.
