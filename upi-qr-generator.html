📱 UPI QR Generator

Enter a UPI ID to generate a payment link.

Verify all payment details in your UPI app before approving a payment.

How the UPI QR is created

The tool builds a standard upi://pay payment URI from the details you enter. If an amount is supplied, the currency parameter is set to INR.

Privacy note: the QR image itself is rendered by the third-party QRServer service (api.qrserver.com). The encoded UPI payment URI is therefore sent to that service to create the QR image. Avoid entering information you do not want transmitted to that provider.

NityaTools does not initiate or confirm a payment. Always check the recipient UPI ID, payee name and amount inside your UPI application before authorizing payment.
