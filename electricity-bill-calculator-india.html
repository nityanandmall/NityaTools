💡 Electricity Bill Calculator India

Electricity tariffs differ by state, DISCOM, connection type and slab. Enter values from your applicable tariff or bill for a closer estimate.

How this electricity bill estimator works

The calculator multiplies electricity consumption in kWh (units) by the rate you enter, adds the fixed charge, and then applies the optional percentage entered under other charges.

Energy Charge = Units × Rate per Unit.
Estimated Bill = (Energy Charge + Fixed Charge) × (1 + Other Charge %).

Why your actual bill can be different

This is a flexible estimator, not a state-specific tariff engine. Indian electricity tariffs can use slabs and may include fixed or demand charges, fuel adjustments, duties, subsidies, rebates, minimum charges and other components that vary by DISCOM and consumer category. Use the current tariff shown by your electricity provider for important calculations.
