Contact NityaTools

We welcome feedback, bug reports, correction requests and suggestions for new tools.

Contact channel is being finalized.
A dedicated NityaTools support email or contact form will be published here. Until then, please do not send sensitive personal, financial, payment or confidential information through unofficial channels.

Reporting a Tool Problem

When the official contact channel becomes available, useful bug reports should include the tool name, the values entered, the result received, the result expected and the device/browser used. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information.

Corrections

If you believe a calculator, formula, conversion, explanatory note or country-specific assumption is inaccurate, please report it so it can be reviewed against an appropriate source.

Suggestions

Suggestions for calculators, developer utilities, image/PDF tools and country-specific tools are welcome. You can meanwhile browse All Tools.
