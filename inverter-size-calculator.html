🔌 Inverter Size Calculator

Estimate the inverter VA rating for your total load.

Enter your load and calculate.

How the estimate works

The calculator first estimates apparent power as VA = Watts ÷ Power Factor, then adds the safety margin you enter.

Example

An 800 W load at 0.8 power factor is 1,000 VA before margin. Adding a 25% margin gives a minimum estimate of 1,250 VA.

Motor, compressor and pump loads can have large starting surges. Actual inverter selection must also consider surge rating, waveform, manufacturer specifications, battery system and the characteristics of connected equipment.
