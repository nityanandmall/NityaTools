Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NityaTools may handle information when you use this website. The site is still developing, and this policy may be updated as features and service providers change.

Information You Enter into Tools

Most calculators and utilities can be used without creating an account. Values entered into browser-based calculators are generally used in your browser to produce the requested result. Where a tool processes files locally, the tool page states that behavior.

File Processing and External Resources

Some file tools use third-party JavaScript libraries delivered through content-delivery networks. For example, current PDF utilities use pdf-lib loaded from jsDelivr. Loading an external library can cause the browser to make a network request to that provider even when the selected document itself is processed locally.

Some features may send encoded information to an external service when necessary for that feature. For example, the current UPI QR generator uses an external QR image service, so the generated UPI payment URI is transmitted to that provider to render the QR image. Review individual tool notices before entering sensitive information.

Technical and Usage Information

Like many websites, NityaTools and its hosting, security, analytics or similar providers may receive standard technical information such as IP-related network information, browser type, device type, referring pages, requested pages and general usage or diagnostic data.

Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

NityaTools may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality and analytics. If advertising is enabled in the future, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement or personalization where permitted. Applicable consent choices and disclosures should be provided when required.

Third-Party Services and Links

The website may use or link to independent third-party services, libraries, content-delivery networks or websites. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, not this policy.

Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to maintain a secure website, but no internet service, browser environment or third-party provider can guarantee absolute security. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into general-purpose tools.

Children

NityaTools is a general-purpose utility website and does not currently provide an account-registration feature intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should follow applicable parental or guardian guidance when required.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as NityaTools develops or as legal, technical and service requirements change. The last-updated date on this page will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. A dedicated support channel is still being finalized.
