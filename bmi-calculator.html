⚖️ BMI Calculator

For general adult screening only; BMI is not a diagnosis.

How BMI is calculated

BMI, or body mass index, compares body weight with height. With metric measurements, the formula is BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in meters².

General adult BMI categories

This calculator labels values below 18.5 as underweight, 18.5 to below 25 as the healthy range, 25 to below 30 as overweight, and 30 or above as the obesity range. These are broad screening categories and do not account for factors such as muscle mass, body composition, age, pregnancy or individual medical circumstances.

Important

Do not use this calculator as a diagnosis or as a substitute for professional medical assessment. Health decisions should consider more than BMI alone.
