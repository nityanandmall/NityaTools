🎓 GPA Calculator

Enter grade points on your institution's scale, separated by commas. This computes a simple unweighted average.

How this GPA calculator works

The calculator adds the grade points you enter and divides the total by the number of values. It therefore calculates a simple, unweighted arithmetic average.

Average GPA = Sum of grade points ÷ Number of grade points.

Weighted GPA and credit hours

This tool does not apply course credits, honors/AP weighting or institution-specific grade conversions. If courses carry different credit hours, a weighted GPA requires multiplying each grade point by its applicable credits before dividing by total credits.

Check your institution's rules

GPA scales and conversion policies vary between schools, universities and countries. Use the grade-point values and calculation method specified by your institution for official academic purposes.
