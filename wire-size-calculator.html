Enter values and calculate.

How this DC wire estimate works

The calculator uses a two-conductor round-trip length and copper resistivity of approximately 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m. It finds the conductor area that keeps calculated voltage drop within the percentage entered.

Why wire sizing needs more checks

Voltage drop is only one part of cable selection. Ampacity, insulation temperature rating, ambient temperature, bundling, installation method, terminals, fault protection and local electrical codes can require a larger conductor.

This is an estimating tool for copper DC conductors, not a substitute for applicable electrical standards or professional design.
