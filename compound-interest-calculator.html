💰 Compound Interest Calculator

Compound interest formula

This calculator uses A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year and t is time in years. Interest earned is A − P.

Why compounding frequency matters

With the same stated annual rate, more frequent compounding can produce a slightly higher future value because previously earned interest is included in later calculations.

This is a mathematical estimate and does not include taxes, fees, changing rates, additional deposits or institution-specific terms.
