🏦 Loan EMI Calculator

Estimate monthly loan payment, total interest and total amount repaid.

How loan EMI is calculated

For a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan, the monthly payment depends on the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments.

Formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate and n is number of months.

What the results mean

Monthly payment is the estimated installment. Total interest is the difference between total payments and the original loan amount. Actual lender charges, fees, taxes, insurance or variable-rate changes are not included unless added separately.
