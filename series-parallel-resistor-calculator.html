〰️ Series / Parallel Resistor Calculator

Enter positive resistor values in ohms separated by commas.

Resistors in series

Series resistances add directly: Rtotal = R1 + R2 + R3 + ….

Resistors in parallel

For parallel resistors, 1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3 + …. The equivalent parallel resistance is lower than the smallest individual positive resistor.

Example

For 100 Ω, 220 Ω and 330 Ω, the series equivalent is 650 Ω. This tool also calculates their parallel equivalent automatically. Practical circuits should also account for resistor tolerance and power ratings.
