Image Resizer

Resize an image to exact pixel dimensions in your browser.

Browser-based image resizing

Your selected image is processed with the browser canvas on your device. The tool resizes to the exact width and height entered and exports the result as PNG.

This version does not automatically lock the original aspect ratio. If the new width-to-height ratio differs from the original, the image will be stretched or compressed. Very large images may also use significant device memory.
